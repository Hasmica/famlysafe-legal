Famly Safe

One secure place for your family's assets, insurance, accounts, and documents.

Famly Safe is a mobile app that helps families keep track of what they own, what's insured, where important documents live, and when things need renewing — shared safely between the people who need to see it.

What it does

A shared family workspace Family members, assets, insurance policies, bank accounts, and documents, all organized in one place.
Real access control Owner and Viewer permissions decide who can see and edit what — enforced at the database level, not just in the app's screens.
Reminders Never miss a renewal or expiry, with reminders that surface right on the home screen.
Your documents, secured Upload and store the documents that matter, with access limited to your own family.

About

Famly Safe is developed and maintained by an individual developer, Hasmica. It does not use AI, does not show ads, and does not sell your data. See the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Data Deletion Instructions for full details.
